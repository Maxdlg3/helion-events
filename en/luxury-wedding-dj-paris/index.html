The formula

DJ + live musicians: the party your guests will talk about

Most weddings have a DJ. Very few have a saxophonist who walks onto the dance floor and plays live over the DJ's house, disco and party classics. That moment, when the whole room turns around, is what Solor is built on.

The founder of Solor, Max, is a saxophonist trained at the conservatoire from the age of seven, who has played the Caveau de la Huchette, Paris jazz clubs and more than a hundred private and corporate events. He selects every DJ and musician himself and is your single point of contact.

FAQ

What international couples ask us.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can plan everything with us in English, by email, WhatsApp or video call, and the DJ and musicians can make announcements in English or French on the night.

Can we book from abroad without visiting Paris first?

Yes. Many couples planning from abroad only meet their suppliers on the day. We plan the music over a video call, share playlists online and coordinate directly with your planner and venue.

Do you travel outside Paris?

Yes. We play in Paris, in châteaux and estates across Île-de-France, everywhere in France and abroad. Travel is quoted transparently, by distance.

Are sound and lighting included?

Yes. Professional sound, lighting, microphones, set-up and take-down are included and adapted to your venue, indoors or outdoors.

How much does a wedding DJ with a live saxophonist cost?

Every wedding gets a tailored quote, depending on the length of the evening, the venue, travel and the musicians you choose. Send us your date and venue and you will receive a detailed quote within 48 hours.

How far in advance should we book?

For a wedding between May and September, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months ahead. For other dates, a few months is usually enough, but get in touch as soon as your date is set.
